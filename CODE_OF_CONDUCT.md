# Code of conduct

MDS contributors and maintainers should make technical collaboration respectful
and usable for people with different backgrounds and access needs.

Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks. Accept correction, explain
tradeoffs and respect reasonable boundaries. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate
against participants or publish another person's private information without
permission. Sexualised harassment and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and project
spaces maintained for MDS. Participants representing the project should follow
the same standards in related public activity.

Report a concern privately to a repository maintainer through an established
project contact channel. Include enough context for review and avoid distributing
private material more widely than necessary. A maintainer who is involved in the
concern should not decide its outcome alone when another maintainer is available.

Maintainers may clarify expectations, remove content, restrict participation or
exclude a participant according to the severity and recurrence of the conduct.
They should explain an action to the affected participant when practical and
protect the privacy of people who report concerns. No response-time guarantee
is implied.
